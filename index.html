Booking Rules for Eyelash Extensions

  • Deposit: A deposit of 100 AED is required to confirm your appointment. This amount is applied to your service and non-refundable in case of late cancellation.
  • Rescheduling & Cancellation: Please reschedule or cancel at least 24 hours in advance. Late cancellations forfeit the deposit.
  • Late Arrivals: If you are more than 20 minutes late, the appointment may be shortened or rescheduled. The deposit will not be refunded.
  • Hygiene: Arrive with clean, makeup-free eyes for best results. Appointments may be rescheduled if you have eye conditions (e.g., conjunctivitis).
  • Children & Guests: Please avoid bringing young children for safety and comfort.
  • Confirmation: Your appointment is confirmed once the deposit is received and you get a confirmation message.
